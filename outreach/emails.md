# Outreach drafts (sent as Huxley Boivin)

**No links in cold emails (since 2026-11-23).** The Gmail connector rewrites every URL into a google.com/url
redirect (see `ops/gmail_link_wrapping.md`), which looks like phishing to corporate filters and shows a
"Redirect notice" after about a day. So templates A, B, C and the follow-up carry no URL and no bare domain.
Instead they offer to send the preview or sample on request. The link goes out in the reply, which the
recipient opens straight away, so the redirect still works. Every email keeps the CASL mailing address
`230 Regina Street North, Waterloo, ON`. Send only to business addresses the company
publishes for this kind of inquiry (business development, partnerships, media/general contact),
and only on topics tied to the person's role. Honour any opt-out right away. This isn't legal advice.
