## Zoning, official plan and by-law changes

### Town of Saugeen Shores: Public meeting on zoning amendment for Neoen 400 MW BESS (Z-2026-028)
*On agenda · Council Meeting - Planning · 2026-11-23 · BESS · 400 MW · Neoen*

Council holds the statutory public meeting on Zoning By-law Amendment Z-2026-028, which would rezone part of a property adjacent to 752 Concession 2 (Saugeen Township) to permit a 400 MW lithium-ion battery storage facility tied to an IESO contract. Staff recommend the report be received for information; agency and public comments, a site plan, a draft amending by-law and an applicant presentation are attached. No decision on the by-law is on this agenda.

> "NEOEN is proposing to establish a 400-megawatt (MW) lithium-ion Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) facility adjacent to 752 CONCESSION 2 (Saugeen Township)"

## Also on the radar

### Township of The Archipelago: Proposed council resolution on Hydro One Sudbury-Barrie line route selection
*On agenda · Council Meeting · 2026-11-20 · transmission · Hydro One*

Item 15.5 carries a proposed resolution on the roughly 300 km, 500 kV Sudbury-Barrie Transmission Line Class EA. It would ask Hydro One to explain how the study area was narrowed, share mapping and GIS data, and consult the Township before a preferred route is chosen, and would state opposition to a route through the Township with significant and avoidable impacts. The agenda shows it as a proposed resolution; the outcome of the vote is not in this document.

> "Council expresses its strong opposition to any proposed transmission route through the Township of The Archipelago that results in significant and avoidable impacts"
