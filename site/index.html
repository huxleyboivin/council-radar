How it works

Nothing to install. One email a week, written to be read in five minutes.

  1. We collect every document

    Each morning we pull new agendas, staff reports, by-laws and minutes from Ontario council portals.

  2. AI reads them, rules check them

    Only passages about energy projects get read. Each item is tagged and summarized, with a quote and a link to the source.

  3. You get Monday's brief

    Grouped by what matters: support granted, refused, requested, new protocols, zoning, early signals.

How accurate is it?
Every item quotes and links its source, so you can check it in one click. The summaries are AI-written from public documents. Confirm the details in the source before you rely on them.
Is this different from a legislative monitoring platform?
It's narrower and much cheaper. We track one thing, council activity on energy projects in Ontario, and deliver it as a brief instead of a dashboard.
