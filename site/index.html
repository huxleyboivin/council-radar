No support yet

Puslinch waits for its own policy before supporting projects

Council told a 7 MW solar proposal it had been informed, but didn't give support. Staff will write an IESO policy in early 2027.

New rules

Haldimand lets staff sign support letters

Projects that meet the county's criteria no longer need a council vote. Wind isn't eligible. Payments are $1,133 per MW a year.

Coming up

Minto expects a wind and solar support request

Prowind has presented 70 MW of wind and 24 MW of solar. Residents have held information sessions. Council hasn't taken a position.

Council support can make or break a proposal

Under the IESO's LT2 and Long Lead-Time procurements, a project on municipal land needs a support resolution from that council. Each council sets its own process, and the decisions are spread across hundreds of agendas and PDF reports.

Questions

Which councils do you cover?

150 Ontario councils today, all listed on the tracker page. We add more every month. If you need a council that isn't listed, reply to any email and we'll prioritize it.

How accurate is it?

Every item includes a short quote and a link to the council's document, so you can check it in one click. Summaries are written with AI from public records, so confirm the details in the source before you rely on them.

How is this different from a legislative monitoring platform?

It's focused and affordable. We follow one thing, council activity on energy projects in Ontario, and send it as a short email instead of another dashboard to check.
