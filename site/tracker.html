Support refused or not yet given 2

  • Council acknowledges a 7 MW solar project but doesn't give support yet

    Township of Puslinch / October 7, 2026 / 7 MW solar / Landowner-led proponent

    Council sent a letter confirming it has been informed of the project, rather than a letter of support. It asked staff to develop a protocol for IESO projects, including community engagement, in Q1 2027, and is writing to the local MPP about how IESO timelines fit with municipal planning. What it means: support letters are unlikely before the protocol is in place, so plan around Q1 2027. Source

  • Council votes against support for 400 MW / 3.2 GWh storage project

    Municipality of Shuniah / December 2025 / 400 MW battery storage / PowerBank (formerly SolarBank)

    Council declined a Municipal Support Resolution for PowerBank's long-duration storage project weeks before the LT2 capacity deadline. Source

New protocols and criteria 3

  • Revised LT2 criteria let the CAO issue support letters; wind is not eligible

    Haldimand County / April 7, 2026

    Council unanimously kept blanket support. The CAO can now issue support letters to projects that meet the criteria, so proponents don't have to come to council one by one. Eligible: ground-mounted solar, battery storage, hydro, biomass, nuclear, natural gas, biogas and co-generation in industrial areas; wind turbines remain excluded. Proponents need a public website at least 3 months before submitting to the IESO, consultation finished 2 months before, and two public meetings with notice to owners within 500 m. A community benefit agreement remains mandatory: $1,133/MW per year in 2026, capped at $300,000 per project. Source

  • Staff propose a faster support process for solar; other projects go to council

    City of Richmond Hill / April 1, 2026

    Staff proposed a formal process for municipal support requests: a blanket support resolution, with delegated authority, for solar projects, and individual council resolutions for every other energy technology. What it means: solar developers may get a faster staff-level path here, while storage and other technologies still need a council vote. Source

  • Municipal Support Resolution protocol for utility installations adopted

    Township of Selwyn / June 24, 2025

    Selwyn adopted a written protocol setting out what proponents must provide when they ask council for a Municipal Support Resolution. Source
