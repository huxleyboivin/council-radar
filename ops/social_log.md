Ontario's Long Lead-Time RFP closes Thursday, Nov 26 at 3 p.m. EPT. The IESO is buying hydro and long-duration storage on 40-year contracts, with a target date of May 25, 2027 to notify selected projects.

A lot of these projects are decided earlier, at the council table. A project on land inside a municipality must include a Municipal Support Confirmation, or the IESO can't consider it. On August 13, Bluewater council voted 9-0 against a 382 MW compressed air storage project aimed at this RFP.

The same rule applies to LT2 Window 2 in 2027, and the councils that took office on Nov 15 will hear those requests.

Council Radar reads Ontario council agendas every day and sends a weekly brief on these decisions. Free guide to the LLT RFP: https://getcouncilradar.ca/guides/long-lead-time-rfp/

### Reddit (energy, r/ontario)
- date written: 2026-11-24 · platform: Reddit (manual, optional) · edition: energy
- links: IESO Window 2 presentation (July 8, 2026), https://getcouncilradar.ca/guides/lt2-window-2/
- status: draft. The subreddit's current self-promotion rules could not be checked this run; read the sidebar before posting and skip it if links to your own site aren't allowed. No housing Reddit draft: a post about a sample issue reads as promotion.
- title: LT2 Window 2: the councils elected in October will decide which energy projects can bid in 2027

I run Council Radar, a small service that reads Ontario council agendas, so I follow this closely. Sharing because it isn't widely known outside the industry.

The IESO's LT2 procurement offers 20-year contracts for new electricity supply. For a project on municipal land, the developer needs a support resolution from that council before it can submit. Without one, the project isn't eligible for a contract.

The IESO's approximate timeline for Window 2: final RFP in Q1 2027, proposals due Q2/Q3 2027, contracts awarded Q1 2028. So the councils that took office on Nov 15 will hear these requests in 2027.

Window 2 also lets existing facilities over 1 MW, like older wind and solar farms, compete to repower for new 20-year contracts, if they meet the IESO's eligibility paths.

Source: the IESO's Window 2 community engagement presentation (July 8, 2026): https://ieso.ca/-/media/Files/IESO/Document-Library/engage/long-term-rfp-community/lt2-rfp-window2-community-20260708-presentation.pdf

I also wrote a free summary with links: https://getcouncilradar.ca/guides/lt2-window-2/

Happy to answer questions about how councils handle these requests.
