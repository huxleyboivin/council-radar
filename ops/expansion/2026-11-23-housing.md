# Expansion memo: Land development and housing feed (Ontario)

Date: 2026-11-23 · Candidate 2 of 7 (run early at Huxley's request) · Verdict: **GO**. The feed is `housing` in `feeds.yaml` (collect-only beta: excerpts are saved daily, nothing is sent). The sample is `samples/housing-sample.md`, and the top-20 buyers and other sectors are in `2026-11-23-housing-buyers.md`.

## Who buys and how many
- Homebuilders: OHBA has 4,000 member companies [1]; 7,094 HCRA-licensed builders/vendors [2]; BILD (GTA) 1,000+ [3].
- Planners: 6,120 OPPI members, 25% in private practice (≈1,500) [4].
- Land-use lawyers: 250+ in the OBA Municipal Law Section [5]; Chambers ranks 15 Ontario zoning/land-use firms [6].
- Rental owners and REITs: FRPO has 2,200+ members [7]; 16 TSX REIT-index constituents [8].
- Demand signal: in 2024-25 the OLT took in 285 OPA appeals, 281 zoning appeals and 149 minor-variance appeals [9]. 50 municipalities have provincial housing targets tied to the $1.2B Building Faster Fund [10].

## What they pay today, and to whom
UrbanToronto Pro (GTHA only, application through council and appeal): $599/month Lite [11]. NRU newsletter: $468/yr [12]. GeoWarehouse: $3,900–7,540/yr [13]. ConstructConnect: $129–199/month per seat [14]. Altus Data Studio (GTA/Vancouver applications) and Urbanation are quote-only [15][16]. Toronto and Ottawa run free application maps [17]. **No paid source covers council decisions across Ontario's 444 municipalities [18].**

## Keywords and councils
The 16 patterns cover OPAs, ZBLAs, plans of subdivision and condominium, site plans, minor variances tied to unit counts, MZOs, development charges (DCs), CIPs, Bills 23/185/17/60 [19][20], housing targets and funding, OLT appeals, servicing allocation, parkland and inclusionary zoning. Bare "CIP" is excluded because councils use it for "Council Information Package". We already read 37 of the 50 large growth municipalities checked, including Ottawa, Mississauga, Brampton, Hamilton, Vaughan and Markham (`municipalities.yaml`). **Toronto is not covered.**

First collection (Nov 23–24, after a re-scan): 134 housing excerpts from 65 councils (`data/excerpts/`).

## Platforms and robots.txt
- **eScribe:** allowed, and already read daily.
- **CiviCweb:** disallowed (119 councils, mostly small) (`data/platform_survey.yaml`).
- **Toronto (TMMIS):** a separate platform; robots.txt has not been checked yet.
- **Attachment 403s:** 709 of 1,623 stored documents show HTTP 403 errors (Ottawa 523, Haldimand 82, Kincardine 57, Bruce 47; `data/radar.db`). *Correction, 2026-11-24:* every one of these is from the very first run on 2026-11-16; no fetch errors since (`data/health.json`). It looks like a one-time block or rate limit, not an ongoing gap. Retry those documents once before launch, and add the per-run error count to `health.json` so a repeat can't hide.
