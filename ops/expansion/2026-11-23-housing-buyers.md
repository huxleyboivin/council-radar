## Other sectors worth a feed after housing (ranked)
1. **Data centres and large loads.** 6,500+ MW of data-centre grid requests, almost 30% of 2024 peak [22]. Bill 40 gives municipalities a "Major Project Validator" role [23]. Effort is low: same eScribe councils, new patterns.
2. **Transmission and Hydro One corridors.** 3 southwest priority lines [24], plus Barrie–Sudbury and 3 more designated in Nov 2025 [25]. Same buyers as energy, so add it to the energy feed rather than build a new one.
3. **Aggregates, pits and quarries.** 6,015 ARA approvals, about 40 new a year [26]; OSSGA has 280+ members [27]. Effort is medium, because many sites are in CiviCweb townships we can't read.
4. **Other provinces.** Alberta has 322 municipalities [28], BC 161 plus 27 regional districts [29], Nova Scotia 49 [30]. Effort is high: new parsers and an unverified platform mix.
