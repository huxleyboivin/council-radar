Subject: Permission to index public CiviCweb agendas for a small research service

Hi,

I run Council Radar, a small weekly brief for energy developers on what Ontario councils decide about
energy projects (https://huxleyboivin.github.io/council-radar/). We read public agendas and minutes and
link every item back to the municipality's own document.

Many Ontario municipalities publish on CiviCweb, and your robots.txt asks automated clients not to crawl,
so we don't. Would you allow a narrowly scoped crawler for public meeting listings and agenda documents?
We would identify ourselves in the user agent, fetch at most one request every few seconds per site,
read only public pages, and stop on request. If there's an API or data-sharing program, we'd use that instead.

Happy to share more details or adjust to whatever limits suit you.
